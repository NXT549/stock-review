STOCK REVIEW 2.3 - INSTALL GUIDE
=================================
(the "just tell me exactly what to click" edition)

You need: a Windows computer and an internet connection. That's it.
You do NOT need Python, you do NOT need to be technical, and nothing
here costs money.


STEP 1 - GET THE FILES OUT OF THE ZIP
--------------------------------------
If you're reading this from inside the zip file, stop and unpack it
first (programs won't install properly from inside a zip):

  1. Find "Stock Review 2.3.zip" (probably in your Downloads folder).
  2. RIGHT-click it.
  3. Click "Extract All...".
  4. Click "Extract".

A normal folder appears with three files in it: this guide, a README,
and "Stock Review Setup.exe".


STEP 2 - RUN THE INSTALLER
---------------------------
  1. Double-click  "Stock Review Setup.exe".

  2. Windows will probably show a BLUE BOX that says
     "Windows protected your PC".

     DON'T PANIC. This is normal. It only means the app is new and
     Windows hasn't seen it before. It is NOT a virus warning.

     -> Click the small text that says  "More info"
     -> Then click the button          "Run anyway"

     You only ever have to do this once.

  3. The installer opens. Just keep clicking the obvious button:
     "Next"  ->  "Install"  ->  "Finish".
     No admin password needed. Leave "Launch Stock Review now" ticked
     if you want it to open straight away.

That's it. You now have "Stock Review" on your desktop and in the
Start menu.


STEP 3 - YOUR FIRST RUN (30 SECONDS)
-------------------------------------
  1. Open "Stock Review" (the desktop icon).
  2. A little welcome and a Tip of the Day may pop up - read or close.
  3. Look at the bottom half of the window. Pick something to review,
     or just leave it on whatever is selected. (Even easier: open the
     "Quick picks" menu at the top and click "S&P 500".)
  4. Click the big green  "> Run review"  button.
  5. Watch the ASCII art while it works. Big lists take minutes -
     the progress bar and timer tell you how it's going.
  6. When it's done, click  "Open report". Done. That file is the
     whole point of the app.

Hover your mouse over any button or box in the app and a little
yellow tooltip will tell you what it does.


WHERE YOUR REPORTS GO
----------------------
    Documents\Stock Review\reports

The "Open report" and "Open folder" buttons take you straight there,
so you never have to remember that path. Want reports somewhere else,
like your Desktop? Use the "Where it lands" picker in the app.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
------------------------
* "Windows protected your PC"  -> More info -> Run anyway (see Step 2).
* Some tickers say FAIL        -> Yahoo rate-limits big runs. Wait a
                                  few minutes and click Run again with
                                  the SAME settings - it resumes where
                                  it left off and fills the gaps.
* You clicked Cancel           -> That's fine. Everything fetched so
                                  far is still written as a partial
                                  report; run again later to finish.
* Nothing works at all         -> Check your internet. The app needs
                                  it to fetch live prices.


TO UNINSTALL
-------------
Windows Settings -> Apps -> "Stock Review" -> Uninstall.
It removes the program, then asks if you also want to delete your
saved reports and settings. Yes = clean sweep, No = keep the reports.
